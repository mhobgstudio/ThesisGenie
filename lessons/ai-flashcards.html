The SOJT Framework

Use this Situation, Objective, Knowledge, Output framework to generate comprehensive flashcards:

The Flashcard Prompt Template

Situation: You are working with an uploaded document that contains
academic material I need to study.

Task: Extract all key concepts from the uploaded document, then generate
comprehensive flashcard questions and answers that cover all key
concepts, topics, and details present in the material.

Objective: Create a complete, exhaustive set of flashcard questions and
answers that can be used for studying and retention.

Requirements:
- Include questions that test different levels of understanding:
  * Definition level — "What is X?"
  * Application level — "How would you use X in scenario Y?"
  * Analysis level — "Compare X and Y. What are the differences?"
  * Evaluation level — "Why is X preferred over Y in context Z?"
- Cover all major topics, concepts, terms, and ideas
- Ensure questions are clear and answers are accurate and concise
- Present in a structured format with clear Q/A separation

Four Levels of Understanding

Good flashcards test more than memorization. Include questions at all four levels:

  1. Definition: "What is the TDMS?" — Tests basic recall
  2. Application: "How would you use Boolean search strings to find papers on climate change?" — Tests practical use
  3. Analysis: "Compare Perplexity and Google Scholar for literature reviews. What are the strengths and limitations of each?" — Tests deeper thinking
  4. Evaluation: "In what situations would you choose Research Rabbit over direct database searches?" — Tests critical judgment
💡 Tip: Always include at least 20% of your flashcards at the Analysis and Evaluation levels. These are the questions that appear most often in exams and dissertations.

Processing Tips

  • Split large documents — Process one chapter at a time for better coverage
  • Include your lecture notes — Upload both the textbook and your notes for comprehensive coverage
  • Request frequency markers — Ask: "Flag flashcards covering concepts that are likely to be examined"
  • Generate progressively — Start with Definition level, then add harder questions

Using AI for Spaced Repetition

After generating your flashcards, you can import them into spaced repetition tools:

  • Anki — Free, open-source, highly customizable
  • Quizlet — Easy to use, has a mobile app
  • RemNote — Combines note-taking with flashcards
💡 Tip: Ask the AI to format the output as a CSV or tab-separated text, making it easy to import into Anki or Quizlet in bulk.
