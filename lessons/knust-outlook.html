Step-by-Step Login

Step 1: Go to Outlook

Navigate to https://outlook.office.com/ or open the Outlook app.

Step 2: Enter Your KNUST Email

Use your full KNUST email address:

  • Regular students: username@st.knust.edu.gh
  • Distance learning: username@idl.knust.edu.gh

Step 3: Enter Your Password

Use your KNUST password — the same one you use for the student portal.

Step 4: Sign In

Click "Sign in" and complete any Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA) if prompted.

💡 Tip: Your KNUST email, student portal, and TDMS all share the same credentials. If you can log into one, you can log into all of them.

Forgot Your Password?

  1. Visit https://apps.knust.edu.gh/students/Account/ForgotPassword
  2. Enter your Student ID (from your admission letter or ID card)
  3. Follow the reset steps via your personal email
  4. Once reset, retry the Outlook login

Checking for Verification Emails

When signing up for services like the GitHub Student Pack, verification emails are sent to your KNUST inbox:

  • Check your Inbox
  • Check Spam/Junk folder
  • Check any filters you may have set up
  • Look for emails from noreply@github.com or similar

Troubleshooting

  • Can't log in? — Reset your password via the forgot password link
  • MFA issues? — Ensure your phone number is up to date in the student portal
  • Emails not arriving? — Contact KNUST IT to unblock external emails
  • Still stuck? — Submit a ticket at helpdesk.knust.edu.gh or visit UITS at the Casely Hayford Block
💡 Tip: This email works for your KNUST classes, Zoom coordination, and all university communications. Keep it active and check it regularly.
