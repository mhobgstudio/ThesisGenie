Using AI as Your Research Assistant for Literature Reviews

Master doctoral-level literature reviews using Perplexity, Brave Search, and AI tools for comprehensive research.

Why Use AI for Research?

AI-powered research tools can dramatically speed up your literature review process. Instead of manually searching dozens of databases, you can use tools like Perplexity, Brave Search, and NotebookLM to find, synthesize, and organize academic sources in a fraction of the time.

The AI Research Assistant Prompt

Here's a powerful prompt template you can use with any AI assistant:

You are an expert research librarian and PhD-level academic investigator
with agentic browser capabilities. Your task is to conduct a
comprehensive, doctoral-level literature review on [TOPIC] by actively
searching and retrieving sources from authoritative academic databases.

Research Databases to Search:
Scopus, Web of Science, ProQuest, JSTOR, SpringerLink, ScienceDirect,
Google Scholar, and specialized databases like PhilPapers or ATLA
Religion Database.

Research Objectives:
- Map the intellectual landscape of [TOPIC]
- Identify seminal works and recent high-impact publications
- Synthesize methodological approaches
- Surface research gaps that could anchor an original thesis argument

Conduct the Following:
1. Active Search — Query each database with Boolean search strings
2. Synthesis — Organize findings into thematic clusters
3. Critical Evaluation — Note contribution, methodology, citation impact
4. Hyperlinked Reference List — APA 7th edition, organized by theme

Boolean Search Strings

Use these variations when searching databases directly:

  • "[TOPIC] AND literature review AND methodology"
  • "[KEYWORD1] AND [KEYWORD2] AND empirical study"
  • "[TOPIC] AND framework AND theoretical"
  • "[TOPIC] AND challenges AND future directions"
💡 Tip: Always use quotation marks for exact phrases, AND/OR for combining terms, and NOT to exclude unwanted results. Most databases support Boolean operators.

Recommended AI Research Tools

Perplexity (Premium Education Package)

Perplexity is an AI-powered search engine that provides answers with cited sources. It's excellent for quick literature discovery and understanding the state of the art on any topic.

Research Rabbit

A free tool that helps you discover related papers through citation mapping. Start with a paper you know, and it will show you connected research you might have missed.

sciSpace

Allows you to interact with research papers — ask questions about them, get explanations of complex sections, and find related work.

NotebookLM

Google's AI research tool. Upload your documents and it can summarize, answer questions, and generate study materials from them.

Organizing Your Findings

  1. Create thematic clusters — Group papers by subtopic, methodology, or time period
  2. Build a citation matrix — Track which papers cite which, to find the most influential works
  3. Note research gaps — What hasn't been studied? What methodology is missing?
  4. Track your search strategy — Record which databases you searched, what terms you used, and how many results you found
💡 Tip: Use the "2 for 1" rule — for every paper you include in your review, find 2 more that cite it. This builds a comprehensive web of scholarship.

Output Structure for Your Literature Review

  1. Executive overview — The field and its major scholarly conversations
  2. Thematic synthesis — Literature organized by major research streams
  3. Methodological landscape — Approaches used across the discipline
  4. Research gaps — Potential thesis angles and original contributions
  5. Complete reference list — APA 7th edition, organized by theme, with DOIs/URLs
