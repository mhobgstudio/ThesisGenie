Ethical Framework: AI as a Tool, Not a Replacement

Before diving into techniques, it's important to understand the ethical context. These techniques are for using AI as a drafting tool — generating first drafts, outlines, and suggestions that you then heavily edit, personalize, and improve. Submitting unedited AI text as your own work is academic misconduct.

The HVP Framework: Humanize, Vary, Personalize

1. Humanize: Add Your Voice

  • Add personal observations: "In my experience reviewing KNUST's TDMS system..."
  • Include specific examples: Reference your own research, your department, your data
  • Use first-person where appropriate: "I argue that..." instead of "It can be argued that..."
  • Add natural hedging: "This seems to suggest..." rather than "This definitively proves..."

2. Vary: Break Predictable Patterns

  • Mix sentence lengths: Follow a long sentence with a very short one. Like this.
  • Vary paragraph length: Some paragraphs one sentence. Others five or six.
  • Use different transition words: Instead of "Furthermore" every time, try "That said," "Surprisingly," "Looking back," or just start a new paragraph
  • Introduce imperfections: A natural rhetorical question, an em dash, an informal aside

3. Personalize: Inject Domain Knowledge

  • Add specific citations: AI-generated text often lacks precise citations — add real ones
  • Reference current debates: "While Smith (2024) argues X, recent work by Jones (2025) suggests Y"
  • Include data: Numbers, statistics, specific findings from your research
  • Show methodology awareness: Discuss why you chose certain approaches
💡 Tip: The single most effective technique is adding specific, domain-relevant details that only someone who has actually studied the topic would know.

The 70/30 Rule

Aim for at most 30% AI-generated content in your final text. Use AI for:

  1. Generating initial structure and outline
  2. Drafting paragraphs you'll then heavily rewrite
  3. Brainstorming ideas and connections
  4. Checking grammar and flow on YOUR writing

Practical Workflow

  1. AI generates draft — Use a detailed prompt to get a starting point
  2. You restructure — Reorganize, add your sections, remove what doesn't fit
  3. You rewrite — Change phrasing, add your voice, insert specifics
  4. You add citations — Every claim needs a real source
  5. You review — Read aloud. Does it sound like you? Would your supervisor recognize your writing style?
⚠️ Important: No technique guarantees passing AI detection. The best approach is to genuinely learn the material and use AI only as a starting point, not as the finished product.
