How to Use KNUST's Thesis Deposition Management System (TDMS)

What is TDMS?

KNUST's Thesis Deposition Management System (TDMS) is the graduate school platform for submitting, managing, and depositing theses/dissertations online. It handles final thesis uploads, examiner assignments (internal/external), report timelines, and graduate approvals. The system became mandatory from March 2025 onwards.

How to Access TDMS

  1. Go to https://thesis.knust.edu.gh/
  2. Log in using your KNUST student credentials (e.g., username@st.knust.edu.gh + password — same as your student portal/Outlook)
  3. Enter the OTP sent to your registered phone/email
  4. If you need to reset your password, visit the student portal
💡 Tip: Your login credentials are the same as your KNUST portal and Outlook email. If you can access your email, you can access TDMS.

Step-by-Step: Submitting Your Thesis

Step 1: Prepare Your Documents

Before logging in, ensure you have:

  • Your final thesis in PDF format
  • Thesis approval form (signed by supervisor)
  • Plagiarism report (if required by your department)
  • Any supplementary materials (data files, appendices)

Step 2: Create a New Submission

After logging in, navigate to the Thesis Submission section. Click "New Submission" and fill in:

  • Thesis title
  • Supervisor name(s)
  • Department and faculty
  • Submission type (Masters/PhD)

Step 3: Upload Your Thesis

Upload your thesis PDF and any supplementary files. The system will validate file sizes and formats before accepting uploads.

Step 4: Assign Examiners

You'll need to propose both internal and external examiners. The system will send them notifications and track their report timelines.

Step 5: Track Your Progress

Use the dashboard to monitor:

  • Examiner report status (pending/submitted)
  • Supervisor approval status
  • Any required revisions
  • Final graduation approval
💡 Tip: Training videos are available via the KNUST E-Learning Centre on YouTube. If you encounter issues, contact the School of Graduate Studies at sgs.knust.edu.gh or the UITS helpdesk.

Troubleshooting

  • Can't log in? — Use the same credentials as your Outlook email. Reset at apps.knust.edu.gh/students/Account/ForgotPassword
  • OTP not arriving? — Check spam/junk folders. Ensure your phone number and email are up to date in the student portal
  • Upload failing? — Check file size limits. Split large PDFs using tools like smallpdf.com
  • Need help? — Submit a ticket at helpdesk.knust.edu.gh or visit UITS at the Casely Hayford Block

Important Deadlines

TDMS submission deadlines align with congregation dates. For March congregations, submissions typically close 2–3 months before. Check the Graduate School website for exact dates each semester.
