AI YouTube Workflow

Here's how to use AI at every stage of YouTube content creation:

1. Scriptwriting with ChatGPT

Use this prompt to generate video scripts:

Write a YouTube video script about [TOPIC].
Target audience: [AUDIENCE]
Length: [X] minutes
Tone: [Casual/Professional/Educational]

Include:
- Hook (first 5 seconds that grabs attention)
- Introduction (what the video covers)
- Main content (3-5 key points)
- Call to action (subscribe, like, comment)
- Outro

Format: Show [VISUAL] | Say [SCRIPT] | On Screen [TEXT]

2. Thumbnail Generation

Use AI image tools to create eye-catching thumbnails:

  • Canva AI: Generate thumbnails with text overlays
  • Midjourney/DALL-E: Create custom artwork
  • Key rule: Faces get clicks — include a face (yours or AI-generated)
  • Text rule: Maximum 5 words on a thumbnail

3. AI Voiceovers

For faceless channels, AI voiceovers work well:

  • ElevenLabs: Most natural-sounding AI voices
  • Azure TTS: Microsoft's text-to-speech, many languages
  • Edge TTS: Free, good quality, multiple voices

4. Video Editing with AI

  • CapCut: Auto-captions, background removal, AI effects
  • Descript: Edit video by editing text — delete a word from the transcript and it removes it from the video
  • Runway ML: AI-powered video editing and generation

5. SEO Optimization

Use AI to optimize your metadata:

Optimize this YouTube video for search:
Title: [YOUR TITLE]
Topic: [DESCRIPTION]

Generate:
- SEO-optimized title (under 60 characters)
- Description (with keywords, first 2 lines crucial)
- 15-20 relevant tags
- Hashtags (3-5)
💡 Tip: Consistency beats perfection. Post on a regular schedule (2-3 times per week) using AI to reduce the production time per video.
